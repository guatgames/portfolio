# Kinal Connections

## Overview

Companies publish employment proposals for Kinal students, and students can send applications directly to those openings.

Students build a public profile with their skills, personal projects, technical area of study and course certificates. Companies keep a presence on the platform and can browse registered students to find candidates for technical roles.

## Context

The project was one of only two selected from the fifth semester of the Informatics evening program at Fundación Kinal, and was presented at Expo Kinal 2026 — the event's 65th anniversary, which required every project to be useful, attractive and focused on Kinal itself.

More than 200 projects were submitted and 56 were presented. Approved teams had four weeks to build before presenting on the day.

## Architecture

Built with modular and component-based architecture: a React, TailwindCSS and TypeScript front end over a Spring Boot and Java 21 backend that exposes a GraphQL API backed by PostgreSQL, with Spring Security and JWT handling authentication.

The team worked under SCRUM, where I acted as Scrum Master — organising the team, assigning work and steering the course of the project.
