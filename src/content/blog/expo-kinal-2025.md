# What Happens at Expo Kinal?

In this event the selected students have the opportunity to create their own project and exhibit it in front of people and many business owners.

## What did I do?
In this Expo Kinal I presented a simple clone of Tetris developed using vanilla JavaScript.

## Why is this experience enriching?
This experience is very important to me because, by exposing myself to large crowds and being compelled to present the developed project to them, I gained the ability to communicate the project development process — how the projects work and how to explain them to people outside the world of IT.
