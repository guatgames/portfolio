# What Happens at This Event?

This is a special event organized by Senacyt (a government institution) and it took place at UGG (Universidad Galileo de Guatemala). Only universities and organizations participated, but thanks to its name, Fundación Kinal was the only school invited to exhibit.

The TIC's Coordinator told my teacher that a group of students was needed to develop their own project, so my team and I were the group selected to participate in this event.

## How did it all happen?
One day the teachers called us in to talk with us. At that moment we felt afraid, because we knew nothing. After they gave us all the information, they asked us to come up with an idea for the event in just three hours. It was very important that the project used AI for something. After thinking about the idea, the group had only two weeks to develop it and present it.

## What did I do?
In this event we developed Promova, a SaaS designed to generate advertising content automatically. Promova is an AI-assisted advertising studio. Upload a product photo, get automatic colorimetry analysis (dominant color, background, and color palette), build an advertising prompt with your brand identity, and generate ad creatives with AI models — all managed through a monthly credit system. And I was the SCRUM Master again, so I decided the course of the project, what work each member of the team would do, and how we would present it.

## Why is this experience enriching?
This is my third important event, but it was the most important one because we were the only team representing Kinal, so we needed to do this project excellently. This experience taught me how to work under pressure, how to work with little time, and how to solve problems along the way.
