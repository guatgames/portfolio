## Direction contract

THESIS: A portfolio as a single editorial proof sheet: the work and the name are the evidence, and the page refuses the neon arcade, floating glass, glow and spectacle it replaces. Structure comes from rules, alignment and scale, not decoration.

OWN-WORLD: Cool neutral greys on paper and near-black; strong dark rules (2px structure, 1px internal), square corners, flat surfaces, no blur, no gradient text, no colored glow. Archivo's width axis supplies masthead-like display; Source Sans 3 handles reading; JetBrains Mono carries code and metadata. Cards become ruled entries, chips become type labels.

COLOR: Every component and glyph is greyscale. Control and availability marks use the same high-contrast ink as their surrounding text; rule tokens are #7c858c/#626c74 on paper and #68727a/#8e979e on night. Technology logos keep their official brand hue; project and blog imagery/video ships in full color because it is the work, not decoration.

STORY: A visitor lands on a name, a role and a clear line to the work; scrolls through a consistently ruled index of about, projects, technologies, writing and contact; and understands that precision is the craft being sold.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left two-thirds: availability as a quiet status line, enormous name set tight, role beneath, two underlined text actions, and a three-column statistics row separated by a single rule. Right third: a flat ruled ledger showing a code specimen under a version stamp. Scroll hint sits on the baseline, not in a pill.

FORM: Chosen form 7 — case-study proof sheet / editorial contact sheet, ordered seventh among grounded directions. Seed key: c2e52cb5. Named raises: alphabet storm's type-as-matter discipline makes the name the structural event; split-flap concourse's fixed-cell alignment governs metadata rows.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
